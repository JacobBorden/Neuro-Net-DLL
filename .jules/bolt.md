## 2024-05-25 - Parallelizing Extended Matrix Operations
**Learning:** Adding `#pragma omp parallel for` to outer loops in `gelu`, `layer_norm`, and row-wise `softmax` in `extended_matrix_ops.cpp` leads to substantial performance gains (e.g. GELU goes from ~2944ms to ~667ms for 5000x5000, layer norm from ~918ms to ~482ms for 500x500 10iters, and row-wise softmax from ~1195ms to ~506ms for 500x500 10iters). OpenMP collapse(2) performs worse on GELU than just parallelizing the outer loop due to thread synchronization overhead for inner loops. Data movement operations like `split_matrix_by_cols` did not yield clear scalable performance improvements and sometimes regressed due to parallel overhead without memcpy, which isn't safe for non-contiguous matrices. We will stick to parallelizing outer loops of math operations.
**Action:** Apply `#pragma omp parallel for` without collapse clauses to the outer loops of compute-heavy math operations like `gelu`, `layer_norm`, and row-wise `softmax`. Do not parallelize `split_matrix_by_cols` or `combine_matrices_by_cols` without ensuring memory safety, as parallel overhead limits gains.
